## Housekeeping
>[!error] What's with the weird symbols?
>When playing a solo tabletop role playing game, I personally use the [Solo TTRPG Notation](https://zeruhur.itch.io/solo-ttrpg-notation) for recording mechanics and separating them from the fiction.

### Legend
- `>` - player actions
- `?` - oracle questions
- `d:` - mechanics rolls
- `->` - oracle results
- `=>` - consequences
- `↪` - my own symbol for meta commentary

## Spring 1, Bread Day, Flooding 
### Early Morning
Finding a way to land turns out to be difficult.
The rain is so thick that Lotus can't see beyond his nose.
From his little spacecraft, he can see, though with some difficulty, the farmhouse. He finds that it's surrounded by other buildings, other than the wilderness. Would probably be best if he introduced himself to the townsfolk before settling down.
Lotus notices a small clearing right next to the farm. He can treat it as a landing spot.
```md
↪ I think I'm gonna take inspiration from a real life event for the name of the spaceship adn the planet Oak Aerie is in. The spacecraft is called Hayabusa, while the planet is named Itokawa. Lotus doesn't know the name of the planet. He will learn it from a townie. Look it up! It's cool!
```
He prepares to land.
As Lotus finally lands on the clearing, he realises he doesn't have an umbrella. You only live once, he thinks, before realising that his living days are over. It's not like he's going to get sick anyway.
Lotus steps out of his spacecraft. He immediately starts looking for signs of life, then, thinking that everyone might be at home because of the weather conditions. He then sets out towards the town.
The smartest move, he thinks, would be to speak to whoever is in charge, so he starts looking for the town hall.
As Lotus explores the town, he sees a lot of shops. All of them seem to be family-owned small businesses. He can't seem to see any big chain stores in town. He finds this to be good. He always loved small shops and would've liked to own one. He thinks that it's amazing how this community manages to run their shops without need to rely on corporations.
```md
↪ Sorry in advance, I don't mean to preach.
```
Lotus finally arrives to the town's plaza. He registers the big, marbled building to be the town hall, on which the name of the town is written: Oak Aerie.
He looks around. He notices a lot of greenery in the plaza, mixed in with pipes...? That stark contrast hits him. The pipes seem to be both the energy and watering system, upon closer inspection. Lotus never thought that nature and technology could coexist to make something this beautiful, aesthetically pleasing, even. He might have to ask about this to a local, if he means to settle here.
Hesitant, he finally knocks the door of the town hall, mind racing and heart beating.

The door opens. A person puts her nose out to see who it would be. Lotus, upon seeing her, feels a pang of familiarity.
"You're really brave, going out in this weather," they say. "Come in."
Lotus silently obeys, still stunned by the weird, familiar feeling he's having.
They sit down and stare at each other for a good minute. Then she finally speaks: "I have never seen you in town. May I ask who you are and where you're from?"
Lotus does register the question, but he's so caught up in his thought about why this person seems so familiar to him that he appears unresponsive.
```md

```
Lotus can't seem to be able to speak. He's so deep in his thoughts that he can't move, like he shut down.
"You should rest a bit. You seem to be very tired and confused," the other person says. "I'll see you in a few hours."
Lotus seems to agree. With this, the other person tells him to follow them, and brings him to a room with a small bed.
"I'll leave you to rest," they say. "Feel free to ask for anything."
As soon as she leaves, Lotus falls asleep.
### Late Morning
```md
↪ A thought occurred to me: in this world, unless they're humans, people can feel the presence of someone else of the same ancestry. I thought it would add a little bit of spice to it. Taking a lot of creative liberties, I know.
```

Lotus is woken up by the smell of tea. He struggles a bit to sit up, the bed sheets too warm and comfortable to leave them, but that cup of tea is just too enticing.
He hasn't slept in a proper bed in years. Up until now, he always slept in his little nook on Hayabusa: just a pillow, a blanket and a place that's comfortable to lie down on. In the early days of his bounty hunting forays, he didn't even have that. Just lying down on cold, hard metal.
As for how Lotus feels after this nap, he'd say he feels recharged. He could probably try and talk to that person again.
He then sets out to look for them. He still hasn't figured out why he could not find the strength to speak to them before. And he still can't make out what was that familiar feeling he felt.
Lotus finds her sitting and typing on her computer's keyboard. At first he thinks he must've been bothering her and distracting her from what he's sure is very important work.
He tentatively approaches the desk. "Um, hello?"
She turns to him for a second: "Oh, you're awake." And she's back typing away.
He takes a deep breath. "Er, I wanted to apologise for my behaviour from earlier. I don't know what's gotten into me, to be completely honest. Usually, I don't have any trouble with speaking," he says, trying to get their attention, but their expression is unreadable.

She turns to Lotus and smiles. "You don't have to worry. Sometimes it happens." Her voice is warm and soft, almost motherly. "My name is [[ttrpg/iron valley/first light/worldbuilding/characters/Hera\|Hera]]." She extends a hand to him.
To this reaction, Lotus also smiles warmly. "Nice to meet you, Hera. I'm Lotus." He shakes their hand gingerly. "Are you the mayor of this town?"
"No, I'm only the secretary, and custodian of this very town hall," she says. "But I can notify that there's someone who's in need of the mayor. I just need you to tell me why."
"I received a transmission on my spacecraft, Hayabusa. It had no sender, no message. It contained coordinates to the old farmhouse in the outskirts, bordering with the forest. I landed in a clearing next to it." Lotus breathes deeply. "I would like to know if it's inhabited, and if not, if I can move into it."
Hera listens carefully to Lotus' story and reasoning. Then they say: "If I'm thinking about the right old farmhouse, it's run down enough that I'm pretty sure nobody currently lives there. It only needs cleaning and fixing. As for moving in it, you will have to ask the mayor." They smile again. "I, for one, would love to welcome you in our community."
"Oooh, a new face!" Suddenly, a voice came from the other room.

"Ah, [[ttrpg/iron valley/first light/worldbuilding/characters/Dylan\|Dylan]]," Hera sighs. "You're also awake. Finally."
"That's Mx. Mayor for you, Hera! You know that by now. I've been mayor for a month now, c'mon!" Dylan pouts. "And since I'm mayor, I can wake up when I want!"
Lotus really doesn't know what to make of Dylan. They seem to be... hyper. Or rather, very enthusiastic. And annoying.
Hera sighs, again. "That's exactly why you should be up earlier, Mx. Mayor. There's a lot of work to do and I can't do that alone." Then, she gestured towards Lotus. "This young man has a question for you, by the way. Will you make time to hear him out?"
Dylan analyses Lotus, who plays with the folds of his skirt. Then he says: "Well then, let's head to my office, shall we?"
Lotus can only nod, because they're already running off towards the door to their office.
"Before you go and speak with Dylan," Hera says before he could even move, "I ask you to make time for a chat with me. Are you up to that?"
"Of course, I'd love to!" Hera's been really growing on Lotus, and he would like to know more about her.
With that, he heads to Dylan's - the mayor's - office.
The room itself is squeaky clean. Either Dylan is really meticulous in keeping it as such, or he never spends time in it. After having a taste of his behaviour, Lotus decides that it's probably the latter.
"Here, have a seat. Make yourself comfortable." Dylan points to the chair in front of what would be her desk.
"Thank you, um, Mx. Mayor...?" Lotus sits in front of them. He notices an amused expression on their face.
"You can absolutely call me Dylan. I just like to mess with Hera." Dylan says. Lotus looks at them, puzzled. "We go way back."
They look at each other, then Dylan resumes speaking: "So, let's talk about why you're here. You're looking to move into Oak Aerie, right? That's the only part of the conversation i got, honestly. Do you mind giving me more details?"
"Of course," Lotus replies. "My name is Lotus. I received a transmission from... no one?" There was info on who sent it, and I'd like to know who did. It contained coordinates pointing to a run down farmhouse next to town." Lotus takes a deep breath, then he keeps talking: "I'd like to move into the farm. I'll take care of the cleaning and the fixing. And also, I found a clearing not too far from the farm where I landed on Hayabusa, my spacecraft."
"Hmm," Dylan, after a bit of reflecting, says: "So you're not from [[Itokawa\|Itokawa]]?"
Lotus has never heard of that name before. "What is that?"
"This planet," Dylan replies. "Its name is Itokawa."
"Then no, I'm not from this planet."

"Yeah. I'm going to head to the farm and make a list of what needs to be fixed." He's already thinking ahead, realising just now about this daunting task he put on himself. "But what was that you wanted to talk about?"
Hera stops and thinks for a second. Then, they say: "About that, I think it's best if you go to the farm and do what needs to be done. It can wait. We'll have more opportunities to talk, of that I'm sure."
"Huh, okay then." Lotus is a little bit confused, but he'll take it anyway. "I'll see you in the coming days." And he makes for the farm.
When he gets there, he immediately makes a mental list of what needs to be fixed: first, he needs to do some cleaning. The place is filthy. He can't produce crops for cooking or brewing, nor keep animals in this filth. Then, he need to paint the interior walls. He wants to make it a habitable and cosy space for himself. When that is done, he'll move on to fixing the water and electricity systems, so that he could bathe, cook and probably automate watering the crops.
