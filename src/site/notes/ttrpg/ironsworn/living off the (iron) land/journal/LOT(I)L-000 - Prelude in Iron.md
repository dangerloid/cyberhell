### Legend
- `>` - player actions
- `?` - oracle questions
- `d:` - mechanics rolls
- `->` - oracle results
- `=>` - consequences

## Setting up the World Truths
### The Old World
```md
? Why did they leave the Old World?
-> The Old World could not sustain us.
```
Their world became broken, and thus they set out for new shores.
They sucked their land of Her life, and didn't have a clue on how to make amends. Crops wouldn't grow, the once crystal clear waters now forever tainted. They had to find a new land, and quick.
Gods help them, for what they found was not better than what they had before.
### Iron
```md
? What's the world's relationship to iron?
-> Inscrutable metal pillars are found throughout the land.
```
Upon arriving on the northern shores - that's how they called this land before it got its name - they found peculiar structures. Pillars. Several meters tall, and unbreakable.
On further inspection, they seemed to be made of metal - of iron -.
Explorers from the first expeditions found out that these structures are scattered across the land. There was no way of escaping them.
They had so great an impact, that they had to name the new land after them. And thus, it was named the Ironlands.
### Legacies
```md
? Are there/were there other populations in the Ironlands?
-> Before the Ironlanders, before even the firstborn, another people lived there.
```
Their explorers, over the years, unearthed ancient ruins, from centuries, perhaps even millennia ago. They contained indecipherable inscriptions and puzzling machinery.
They spawned a new generation of scholars trying to understand this ancient civilisation, and what was its ruin.
### Communities
```md
? How are communities organised in the Ironlands?
-> We have forged the Ironlands into a home.
```
Through trial and error, they finally settled in the Havens. From there, the small settlement comprised of tents slowly began to grow in a village, and more villages followed suit. Trade routes started to appear, and that enabled for some form of travel.
### Leaders
```md
? Who are the leaders of the Ironlands?
-> Each of our communities has its own leader, called an overseer.
```
Establishing a system for leadership was harsh and bloody, and protracted for way too long. They decided that each settlement needed some sort of guidance, an overseer, or, in any case, someone who could rule over the village. They get chosen every seventh spring by the villagers, and whoever is overseer for the following seven springs, bears an iron circlet, signifying their leader status.
### Defence
```md
? What are the means of defence in the Ironlands?
-> The wardens are our soldiers, guards and militia.
```
There were too few people to establish a full blown army. Full-scale warfare isn't possible in the Ironlands. The weather is just too unkind.
The escamotage the overseers of the Havens came up with for some measure of defence, was to create small scale bands with trained soldiers, serving as guards for the village entrance, and solving feuds with other villages, among other things. The wardens, they called them.
With that, some people thought it beneficial to sell their swords. They are called free wardens, and are, in essence, mercenaries.
### Mysticism
```md
? What is the world's relationship to magic?
-> Magic is rare and dangerous.
```
They found a strange energy coursing through the Ironlands. One could attune to it, with a cost. One had to be preternaturally gifted in the mystic arts to even attempt doing so.
Mysticism isn't what fairy tales and novels tell. 'Tis a perilous art, not to be incautious with.
Mystics are few and far between, and most of the time, they aren't there with their minds.
### Religion
```md
? What is the relationship to the gods in the Ironlands?
-> The gods have forsaken us, but some proof of their existence may yet be there, tangible. Most Ironlanders still pray to them, to no avail. So long as they can fuel their holier-than-thou attitude.
```
The Ironlanders are very much affectionate towards their deities. 'Tis but a one sided relationship. Not too long before they set sail for what would be named the Ironlands, the gods abandoned them, and to this day they show no signs of granting them their blessings again. Not that they deserve them, after destroying the Old World.
### Firstborn
```md
? Who are the firstborn?
-> The firstborn live in isolation.
```
The firstborn, their neighbours. Fierce defenders of their lands and of nature as a whole. They were here before the Ironlanders even thought of leaving the Old World. Perhaps they were always here.
They live in isolated settlements in the wilderness, not to be seen by Ironlanders. They're peaceful peoples, but will react if their lands are threatened.
### Beasts
```md
? Are the beasts an active threat for the Ironlanders?
-> Monstrous beasts stalk the wild areas of the Ironlands.
```
They thought that monstrous beasts were nothing but legends, but an expedition in the wilderness found a fanged beast of ungodly proportions.
Thankfully, beasts such as these are only common in wilder, untamed areas of the Ironlands. There is no risk of them preying on a settlement.
### Horrors
```md
? What horrors lurk in the Ironlands?
-> Monsters lurk in the dark areas.
```
There have been stories of unholy creatures lurking in the dark.
As long as one does not venture too far from their home at night, nothing should happen. But be wary of the shadows.

Or at least, that is what she was told. She will have to see for herself.

## Setting up the Character Truths
### Name, Personality, Backstory
Zura was always a sheltered child. Her elders have never wanted to expose her to the horrors of life in the Ironlands.
Now that she's an adult, she's starting to question everything. She wants to go out there, and explore this supposedly dangerous world she lives in.
Zura has such an optimistic outlook on life, her mother fears it's going to get her killed someday.
```md
? What's the name of the fallen ancient civilisation?
d: 1d100 = 89
-> Ishanan Empire, Ishana, Ishanans
```
She's an avid reader, and an aspiring scholar. Zura has an ever increasing interest in the Ishanan Empire. She really does want to know what happened to them, and how to implement their knowledge to improve the quality of life in her small village. Possibly the entire Ironlands. She can really be ambitious.
Zura is extremely empathetic and compassionate. She can even cry if an object she was attached to breaks.
Her sheltered life prevented her from honing her combat skills, even though she watched the wardens train every morning. She did ask to be trained, but the chief warden always refused her, to avoid the ire of her mother, the overseer.
```md
? Name of Zura's village [Settlement: Name]
d: 1d100 = 45
-> Roll on [Creature] table
d: 1d100 = 29
-> Wolfcrag
```
### Stats

Zura is compassionate and empathetic, but she's also charismatic and persuasive. She lacks physical prowess, but is moderately skilled in the mystic arts, and also very well read.

Zura's first bond could be obvious to some, but it shouldn't be taken for granted, for her mother, Milla, is quite indifferent to her presence in her life.
Milla is also a very busy woman, for she was elected overseer of Wolfcrag last spring, and has now papers to write, meetings to attend to, the works.
And either way, she always favoured her brother...
```md
~ Zura's brother
? Name
d: 1d100 = 78

```
The elder, stronger, and through her mother's eyes, better sibling, Reema, is Zura's second bond.
He taught her how to read, how to research. He defended her from some bad actors when she was still attending the village's study house, so much so that now Zura is seen with a moderate amount of respect.
They love each other dearly. They've been through many hardships together, like their father parting ways with them. She doesn't even remember his name; she just remembers the shape of his silhouette as he walked out the door.
And the third bond, the wardens of Wolfcrag. To Zura, they are a beacon of chivalry and courtesy and heroism and- That is to say, she absolutely swoons over them.
They also really care for her. They never agreed to train her because of her mother, yes, but also because they care deeply about her well being, and such a perilous life is not fit for her, as the chief warden would say.
### Assets

Zura has quite the collection of mystic texts, among other things. She studied them thoroughly, but never really felt the need to embrace mysticism and by so, becoming a mystic. The village already has one anyway.
```md
? Pick asset 2
-> Storyweaver [Path]
```
She's also quite skilled at writing fiction and poems. She can conjure an entire world just by speaking a few words. And everyone around her, listening, would just hang from her lips.
```md
? Pick asset 3
-> Raven [Companion]
```
Her trusty animal companion, the raven Poe, has been with her since she was but a child. He would sit with her while she studied, or read, or researched. Generally, he would enjoy 'aiding' her in her studies.
### Equipment and Items
In Zura's backpack there's a small collection of mystic texts, just enough to keep her occupied when she needs to stall, or during slow moments, such as travelling on a carriage or caravan or resting in a makeshift camp or in a new community. There's also a vial of ink, some paper and a quill, for recording her experiences.
She wears a vest made out of thick cotton, and some pelts that her brother gifted her to withstand the cold and bitter winters. She also always wears iron earrings, the last remnant of her father, and relics from the Old World.
Zura keeps a small knife in her boot. For self defence, although she can't fight, and in case she needs to do manual labour, like carving for example, though she doesn't have any idea on how to do it. I guess I'll learn in time, she thought, putting the knife in its place.

### Background Vow
Zura's dream, ambition, goal in life is to see the world.
Her mother forbade her to ever leave Wolfcrag, and, to avoid angering her, the villagers complied in never letting her venture out of the village entrance.
Now she's an adult. She can make her own choices. Not too long ago, she packed her things in her small leather backpack and confronted her mother about it. Needless to say, she got a firm no. But she's determined enough to see her dream fulfilled.
With her mother as witness, after a long fight, she swore an iron vow to herself. She took out one of her earrings, and held it close to her chest, her heart. She recited the words she rehearsed countless times, in preparation for this moment: "Upon this piece of iron, Old World relic, I vow to explore the Ironlands, north to south, mountains and plains, oceans and rivers. I swear to see this effort complete, and return to Wolfcrag with new knowledge of our world."
This whole ordeal left her mother stunned. Milla didn't think Zura would have the guts to swear on iron. But she did. And Milla will hold her to it.
```md
> [Swear an Iron Vow] (No rolls for background vow)
=> Explore the entire Ironlands and return to Wolfcrag with knowledge of the world. (Epic)
```
### Inciting Incident
Zura is cataloguing her inventory of texts, her mind focused on the task at hand, when she hears the clashing of metal.
She looks out the window of her modest room. Raiders. Not any petty raiders, though. The Lilac Spirits, the fearsome and violent band that terrorises this side of the Havens.
What are they doing here? What could they possibly want from us? Zura keeps asking herself questions, not finding any answers.
Wolfcrag is but a small peaceful community. Yes, attacks from petty raiders can be frequent at times, but the wardens always manage to fend them off with relative ease. But other than that, there isn't anything worth of note for a band of raiders such as the Spirits.
Then, as she watches, she notices it. That familiar silhouette that haunts her memories.
She rushes out of her house, and yells at the top of her lungs: "Father!"
```md
~ Zura's father
? Name
d: 1d100 = 14
-> Kova
```
The man turns to look at her. "Zura? Is that you?"
Zura starts sobbing, as if she regressed to toddler age again. Then, behind her tears, she speaks: "What happened? Why did you leave us? I deserve to know!" She sighs. "I don't even remember your name!"
He keeps fighting. He pierces a warden with his sword, which he then removes with a slick movement of his right arm. The warden dies on the spot.
"It's Kova," he says, cleaning his blade with a cloth.
"Ah, Father. You're finally here. Took you enough," a voice rings behind her. Reema.
Zura takes a moment to realise it. It was an organised attack. From within.
"Brother, what's the meaning of this? You knew. This is treason, you know that! What would mother think?"
Reema cups her face, and gives her a kiss on the forehead. "I don't give a damn about what mother thinks. Truly. She brought this upon herself."
Zura doesn't know what to make of this. Her father is back. He killed one of the wardens in front of her. He holds a sword, indicating he's a high rank raider in the Spirits, maybe even the leader. He organised an attack on Wolfcrag, and her brother is involved.
Then, Kova speaks: "Well done, everyone. They're now intimidated enough. Let's head back to base. Milla's nowhere to be seen anyway."
They're after mother, Zura thinks. She should find her and warn her...
But, where is she? Why didn't she dispatch our forces earlier? And, to add insult to injury, is she cowering somewhere else, waiting for the attack to die down? Why didn't she protect the village herself?
"Reema, you're coming with us," Kova says, sternly.
"Of course, father," Reema replies. Zura has never seen so obedient. What got into him?
After the raiders left, she gets out of her small hiding place next to the family house and goes looking for Milla.
"Mother." Zura finds her in a bush, just behind the smithy. She's trembling.
"Ah, it's you." Milla tries to regain composure, but fails. "Help me get out. And be quick."
"Mother. What does this mean?" Zura begins asking, while helping her with getting out of the bush.
"You and your godsdamned questions, your godsdamned curiosity." As soon as she's up on her feet, Milla gives Zura a cold stare. "This is none of your business."
Zura sighs, then calmly says: "Wolfcrag is also my home. If there's anything I can do to help, I'd be glad to."
"And what is it that you could do, exactly? You can't fight. You focused only on reading your nonsensical books and writing children's stories."
Zura now only sees red. "The only reason I'm unable to fight is you, and your stupid restrictions you put in my life. I'm going to take this matter into my own hands." She takes the other earring, and begins to recite a vow. "I swear upon this iron earring that I will find out the raiders' motivations for attacking us." Zura breathes. "I swear to you that I will succeed."
```md
> Find out the reason for the raiders' attack. [Swear an Iron Vow]
d: 1d6 +heart +bond vs 1d10, 1d10 = 3+3+1(6) vs 10, 8
=> Zura vows to find out the reason why the raiders attacked, but she finds complications. -2 momentum.
```
Suddenly, Zura's legs give out. She can't seem to be able to get up on her own, or walk. Milla goes to call for help.
She returns with a warden, who picks Zura up and brings her home.
At home, she's put on her bed, her mind racing. How am I to fulfill my vows if I can't walk?, she thinks to herself.
Milla sits next to her. "I called a healer to see that you're able to stand on your own two feet in a couple of days. I want you to see your vows fulfilled. Sometimes it does look like I don't care about you, and I want to apologise for that. Everything I ever did, all the 'stupid restrictions' I put on you, were only in your best interest."
"I don't believe you." Zura doesn't even take off her eyes from the book she's reading. "You never cared. But now that I might have some semblance of usefulness to you suddenly you care?"
"I've always cared, I-"
"You're lying. Spare me, I'm begging you."
Milla changes her expression. "You were always an ungrateful child. I should've kicked you out of Wolfcrag long ago," she says, before leaving Zura's room.
Zura puts the book on her bed stand, and silently starts crying.
As she almost falls asleep, she hears a knock to the door. "I'm the healer. Please, let me in."
"Come in," Zura says. She might finally understand why she suddenly lost the ability to walk.
The healer sits on the bed, next to her. "Tell me, child. What happened?"
"I saw a man die, impaled by a sword. It was my father's doing. I've never seen this much violence all at once."
"You're clearly in shock. I think that might be the reason your legs gave out. Here," the healer whips out a small vial. "Take a sip twice a day, and you'll be back on your feet in approximately two days."
"Thank you." Zura immediately takes a sip. "I have a lot of matters to attend to. I need to walk."
"And you will, but be patient." They get up, and make for the door. "I'll take my leave then."
Zura waits for them to leave, then takes a deep breath and attempts to fall asleep.
